6 · Goal Planner

Goals fund in priority order — #1 is first in line. Use ▲▼ to re-rank, tap ⚙ to flip a card over for its settings (target, saved, pull %). All goals share one 100% pool per bucket — inputs stop at what's available. When a goal completes, its pull % spills over to the next goal in line automatically, so you never re-juggle percentages. Reserves: give a bucket a Reserve $ target and its weekly money refills that stash FIRST — goals only see the overflow once it's full. Hit "−" to log a spend from a reserve; refilling restarts and every timeline adjusts.
